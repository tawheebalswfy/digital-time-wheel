# V2 Exit Research

Loss geometry is the principal measured failure: recent forward DEMO planned R:R was about 0.27, and retained historical simulations show frequent small wins with larger losses. Long holding buckets are also negative, but duration may be an outcome marker rather than a causal exit rule.

The fixed-R, stop-cap, maximum-hold, and breakeven grids are registered in `V2_STRATEGY_DESIGN.md` and must be evaluated one category at a time on new, uninspected genuine MT5 history. No R value, stop cap, hold limit, or breakeven threshold has been selected. Cap_1 remains a damage-control reference only: final PF 0.551 and expectancy -0.426, still failing acceptance.
